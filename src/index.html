FAQ

What email providers are supported?
We’ll support Gmail (including Google Workspace), Outlook/Office365, and Exchange via OAuth. Full list coming at launch.
How many emails do you index?
Up to 10,000 of your most recent messages by default; you can scope and re-index as needed.
Is my data used to train models?
Default behavior is NOT to use personal mail to train models without explicit opt-in. See Privacy for details.
Can it send emails or act on my behalf?
Early versions focus on search, summarization, and generation. Any sending or automated actions will be opt-in and clearly labeled.
How accurate are answers?
We ground answers in retrieved source passages and always link back to the original emails so you can verify. For mission-critical items, double-check the original message.

Privacy — short, clear summary

We value your privacy. This is a concise default posture for the landing page. Engineering & legal should finalize the full policy before wide distribution.

Default data use

By default, we index up to 10,000 of your most recent emails solely to provide product functionality. We do not use personal email content to train global models without explicit opt-in.

What we store

Metadata (sender, subject, date), message content required for semantic indexing (vectors and references). You control scope and deletion.

Deletion & export

Users can request data export or deletion. Dashboard will include one-click deletion and scope controls prior to GA.

Security — high level

This page is a high-level overview. Engineering will publish a detailed security whitepaper covering encryption, keys, and audits before enterprise onboarding.

Authentication

We use OAuth. We never ask for raw passwords. Scopes are minimized to least privilege required to index selected messages.

Encryption & audits

TLS for transit; encrypted at rest. Planned SOC2 and penetration testing prior to general availability.
